The manuscript's computational figures, with the manuscript's figure numbers and captions, drawn from the outputs of np-mt-rnm (scripts/run_all.py) on the 146-node, 353-interaction network. Ensembles use 100 replicates from random initial states drawn with MATLAB's own generator and the per-replicate seeds of RESCUE_NEW4_1_48_targets_HQ.m; the rescue percentages match the manuscript to the printed decimal. Each figure can also be viewed as the manuscript-style image generated by scripts/run_paper_figures.py.

Δi = x̄iNL − x̄iHL, 95% percentile bootstrap CI (Nboot = 10,000). A "+" benchmark is concordant when the CI lower bound exceeds 0, a "−" benchmark when the upper bound is below 0; the opposite sign is contradictory and a CI that includes 0 is indeterminate. There is no tolerance band.
